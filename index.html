Starting a new draft? This ranks every player by value: projected points over the next 6 matches, minus what a freely available player at the same position would score. Updated every gameweek.

Value shows how many more points a player should score than the best player still left at his position once every team has filled its starting spots. Big value means drafting him early matters. Players with similar value form a tier, marked with a line.

How the score works

Every player gets a score from 0 to 100, compared with others in the same position. Four things go into it, weighted like the bar shows.

Then I go through the list by hand every gameweek. Numbers miss things like new penalty takers, rotation and injury news, so players get nudged up or down, with a note on why.
